Common questions

My purchase didn't appear.
Open the store from the coin or hearts counter at the top of any screen, then tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

How do lives work?
You start with 5. One regenerates every 2 hours. A life is only spent when you fail a level, never when you start one.

Does Vialo need an internet connection?
No. Everything plays offline.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. All progress is stored only on your device and nowhere else.
