Privacy Policy — Vialo

Last updated: September 2026

Vialo does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, level completion, coins, lives and settings are stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple, this data may also be stored in your own private iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Advertising

Vialo shows optional rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch in exchange for extra lives or coins. Ads are never forced and there are no banner ads.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers, general usage information and diagnostic data. We do not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so ads shown in Vialo are non-personalised and based on general context rather than your activity elsewhere.

Google's own privacy policy covers how they handle this data: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase validation is handled through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt in order to confirm the purchase is valid. Apple's privacy policy governs the payment itself.

Children

Vialo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can stop all data associated with the app by deleting it. If you signed in with Apple, you can revoke access at any time in your device settings under your Apple Account, then Sign in with Apple.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
