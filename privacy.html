Privacy Policy — Vialo

Last updated: August 2026

Vialo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, level completion, coins, lives and settings are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children
